01Introduction

My Time: Tasks & Planner (“My Time”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a productivity app that helps you organize tasks, write notes, set reminders and alarms, track your progress, and get help from an AI assistant.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your tasks, notes, and chats stay on your device, and we do not sell your personal information.

02Information we handle

Stored only on your device

  • Tasks and notes: titles, descriptions, start times, deadlines, priorities, alert type, completion history, and your notes.
  • AI chat history and AI memory: your conversations (including photos you attach) and a short “memory” of facts and topics the AI tutor keeps to personalize answers. You can switch memory off or clear it in the app.
  • Preferences: language, theme, and notification, polish and memory settings.

This data stays on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. If Android Backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include this data in your Google account backup, under Google’s own terms.

Sent to our servers when you use certain features

  • AI features: the text you type, photos you attach, and voice recordings you make for voice input are sent to Google’s Gemini AI service to produce an answer, a transcript, or a cleaned-up task. Your request passes through our own secure server, which checks that it comes from the genuine app and then forwards it to Gemini. We do not store the content of your requests on that server.
  • Anonymous app statistics and diagnostics: a random installation ID (not linked to your name, email or phone number), device brand and model, Android version, app version, app language, which screens are used and how often, feature-use counters, time spent in the app, whether reminders and alarms are working, and error and crash reports (which can include technical stack traces). Task titles, notes, and chat text are never part of these reports. The app signs in anonymously to our Firebase service to send them.
  • Online presence: while the app is open, a small anonymous record (installation ID, current screen, device model, app version) is refreshed so we can see how many people are using the app.
  • Notifications and announcements: the app registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you announcements. Your language choice is used to send announcements in the right language.
  • Remote controls: the app reads a setting from our servers that lets us pause the app for maintenance or safety reasons.
  • Integrity checks: to protect our AI service from abuse, Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check confirm that the app is genuine and running on a normal Android device. This does not give us access to your personal data.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, according to Google’s policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

03How information is used

We use information to:

  • Create, organize, edit, and manage your tasks and notes.
  • Schedule and deliver reminders, alarms, and notifications.
  • Show progress, weekly and monthly recaps.
  • Answer your questions in the AI chat, transcribe your voice, create tasks when you ask the AI to, and polish task text when that setting is on.
  • Remember useful details between AI chats, if you leave AI memory on.
  • Understand how the app is used, fix bugs and crashes, and check that reminders and alarms work reliably.
  • Show ads that help keep the app free, and offer optional rewarded ads.
  • Send announcements and app update notices.
  • Protect the app and our services from abuse.

We do not use your tasks, notes, or chats for advertising.

04Data storage & third-party services

Your tasks, notes, chats, and memory are stored locally on your device. Data that leaves your device is processed by these services, each under its own privacy policy:

Google Gemini (Google AI)Processes AI requests: text, photos, voice.
Google FirebaseCloud Functions, Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging and App Check: our secure AI gateway, anonymous sign-in, statistics and diagnostics, announcements, and abuse protection.
Google Play servicesPlay Integrity, in-app updates and in-app reviews: integrity checks, update prompts, and the rating card.
Google AdMobAdvertising.

Information may be processed on servers outside your country.

05Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, as needed to provide the features you use, and when required by law or to protect the security of the app and its users. Anonymous statistics and crash reports may be viewed by our team through internal monitoring tools.

06Data retention & deletion

  • On your device: you control it. Delete tasks, notes, chats, and AI memory inside the app, clear the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • AI requests: we do not keep the content of your requests. Google processes them under its own retention rules.
  • Anonymous statistics and diagnostics: kept only as long as needed to measure usage, fix problems, and keep the app reliable. Uninstalling the app stops new data from being sent.
  • Requests: to ask about or request deletion of data tied to your installation, contact us at the address below. Because the data is anonymous, we may need your installation details to find it.

07Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app, including encrypted connections, keeping AI service keys on our server instead of inside the app, and checks that block unauthorized use of our services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

08Children’s privacy

My Time is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will work to delete it.

09Third-party services

The services this app relies on are Google Gemini, Google Firebase, Google Play services, and Google AdMob, as described in Section 04. Their data practices are described in Google’s Privacy Policy and the AdMob policy.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version is always published at this URL, and the “Last updated” date will change.
