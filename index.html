01 Introduction

My Time: Tasks & Planner ("My Time", "we", "our", or "us") is a productivity application designed to help users organize tasks, manage schedules, set reminders, and track their activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application.

02 Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, My Time may handle:

  • Task and Schedule Data: Task titles, descriptions, deadlines, priorities, schedules, and reminder settings.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as language, theme, and notification preferences.
  • Device Permissions: Notification and alarm-related permissions needed for reminders.
  • Optional Features: Content you submit to optional features, such as AI-powered tools, may be processed to provide the requested functionality.

The information handled depends on the features available in the version of the application you use.

03 How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create, organize, edit, and manage tasks.
  • Schedule and deliver reminders and notifications.
  • Save application preferences.
  • Display productivity summaries and weekly or monthly recaps.
  • Provide features you choose to use.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the application.

04 Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Task information and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Some features may require information to be processed by external service providers. For example, content submitted to an AI-powered feature may be sent to the relevant AI provider to generate a response.

External providers may handle information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Relevant third-party services should be identified in this policy.

05 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide features you choose to use, comply with legal obligations, or protect the application's security.

06 Data Retention & Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application. Information handled by third-party providers may be subject to their retention and deletion practices.

For privacy-related requests, contact us using the details below.

07 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08 Children's Privacy

My Time's intended audience and handling of children's information are subject to its Google Play target-audience settings and applicable privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable laws.

09 Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party services for certain features. This policy should identify the services actually integrated into the application and explain their relevant data practices.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
