# mytime-privacy
1. Introduction

My Time: Tasks & Planner ("My Time", "we", "our", or "us") is a productivity application designed to help users organize tasks, manage schedules, set reminders, and track their activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, My Time may handle the following information:

Task and Schedule Data: Task titles, descriptions, deadlines, priorities, schedules, and reminder settings that you enter into the application.
App Preferences: Settings such as language, theme, and notification preferences.
Device Permissions: The application may request notification and alarm-related permissions to deliver scheduled reminders.
Information Submitted to Optional Features: If the application offers AI-powered features, the messages, prompts, images, or other content you submit may be processed to provide the requested functionality.

The information handled depends on the features available in the version of the application you use.

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by My Time may be used to:

Create, organize, edit, and manage tasks.
Schedule and deliver reminders and notifications.
Save your application preferences.
Display productivity summaries and weekly or monthly recaps.
Provide additional features you choose to use.
Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application's functionality.
4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Task information and application preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Some features may require information to be processed by external service providers. For example, if AI-powered features are available, submitted content may be transmitted to the relevant AI provider to generate a response.

External providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Any third-party services integrated into the application will be identified in this policy where applicable.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate features you choose to use, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect the security of the application, or address other circumstances permitted by law.

The information shared depends on the functionality used and the services integrated into the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Information processed or stored by third-party providers may be subject to their respective data retention and deletion practices.

If you need assistance with data deletion, you can contact us using the details provided below.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children's Privacy

My Time is a productivity application. Its intended audience and handling of children's information are subject to the application's Google Play target-audience settings and applicable privacy laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

Where applicable, users can consult the privacy policies of the relevant providers to understand how those providers handle information.

This policy should be updated whenever third-party integrations or the application's data practices change.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, services, or data practices change.

Any revised policy will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with an updated effective date where appropriate.
